| T-12-TOKENLEAK | Information Disclosure | merge-preview proxy + actions | mitigate | high | ADMIN_TOKEN and FASTAPI_BASE_URL imported only from `$env/static/private`; the proxy returns FastAPI JSON without echoing the token; no PUBLIC_ env var introduced (CLAUDE.md Rule 2) |
