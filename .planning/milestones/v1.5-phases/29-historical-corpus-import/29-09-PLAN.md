<objective>
Close the single remaining BLOCKER gap (CR-01) from 29-VERIFICATION.md: the corpus importer's Argument-dedup check is keyed on `Argument.oyez_transcript_id`, but the `arguments` table's real DB uniqueness constraint is `(source_docket, question_number)`, and every corpus Argument hardcodes `question_number=1`. Reargued cases and dockets already ingested by the PDF pipeline therefore collide at `session.flush()`, raise an `IntegrityError` caught only by a blanket `except Exception`, and are silently folded into `conversations_errored` — indistinguishable data loss.

Purpose: Make the bulk historical import reliable for the real corpus (reargued cases across 1955-2019 and any docket overlap with prior PDF ingestion) and make any residual collision operator-visible in the per-batch summary rather than silent (CORPUS-03 reliability, CORPUS-08 summary visibility).

Output: Per-docket `question_number` derivation, a distinct `docket_question_conflict` counter surfaced in the summary, explicit `IntegrityError` handling as a defense-in-depth safety net, and regression tests proving the two documented collision scenarios no longer silently drop data.
</objective>
