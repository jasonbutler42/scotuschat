  <what-built>
Two fix plans landed since the last checkpoint.

**Bio saving (Plan 39-07).** The Bio field now belongs to the **Save Person** button like every
other person field. Before, it was only submitted by the **Upload photo** button, so typing a bio
and pressing Save Person sent everything except the bio — and nothing said so. The Photo form no
longer writes person fields at all, and the Bio box now always shows what is actually stored, so a
failed save can no longer look like a successful one.

**Popover styling (Plan 39-08).** The separator now has real space on both sides. Every section of
the card is separated by a hairline rule, as in the mockup. Each tenure is now two columns: the
office title in bold on the left, its date range right-aligned on the same line, then the
appointing president and party on the left with the reason right-aligned beneath. Tenure ranges now
show month and year (`Sep 1986 – Sep 2005`) instead of years only.
  </what-built>

**1. Save a bio.** Open a Justice in the admin people editor. Type a few sentences into the Bio
box. Press **Save Person**. Reload the page.

- The bio is still in the box.
- Note that the Bio box now shows the hint "Saved with Save Person."

**2. See it on the card.** Open an argument that Justice sat on. Click their avatar.

- The bio paragraph is on the card.

**3. Exercise the Read more toggle.** Use a Justice whose bio is long enough to be cut off.

- The paragraph stops after three lines.
- "Read more" appears. Click it. The full text shows and the link becomes "Show less".
- Click again. It collapses.
- On a Justice with a short bio, there is no "Read more" link.

**4. Check the separator spacing.** On any Justice with a death date, look at the birth/death
line, then at the president/party line in the tenure block.

- Both separators have clear space on each side.
- Compare against `popover - Bench.png`. The spacing should read the same.

**5. Compare the card to the mockup.** Open `popover - Bench.png` beside a two-tenure Justice's
popover (Rehnquist is the example the mockup was drawn from).

- Each tenure is one bold title on the left with its date range right-aligned on the same line.
- Beneath it, president and party on the left, reason for leaving right-aligned.
- A hairline rule sits between each section: after the name block, after the birth/death line,
  after the bio, above the tenures.
- No rule between the two tenure blocks.
- No dead space under the last tenure.
- Date ranges read as month and year, for example `Sep 1986 – Sep 2005`.

**6. Check party-neutral rendering again.** This matters because the bold weight is new. Open one
Justice appointed by a Democratic president and one appointed by a Republican president.

- Same field order, same sizes, same grey on both.
- The only bold text in the tenure block is the office title. The president, the party and the
  reason are all the same weight and colour as each other.
- Nothing is red, blue, bolder, or marked differently because of the party named.

**7. Check the two save forms still leave each other alone.** On a Justice who has a photo, a bio
and a death date:

- Save a photo. Reload. The bio and the death date are both still there.
- Change the death date and press **Save Person**. Reload. The photo and the bio are both still
  there.

**8. Confirm the deliberate differences.** These five things differ from the mockups on purpose.
Say whether each is fine, or that you want it changed:

- No "Edit person" link. Deferred to backlog item 999.9.
- The advocate card's descriptor reads "Coming soon" in italics instead of a location. No data
  source exists for it yet.
- The advocate card's role pill is blue; the mockup's is grey.
- The avatar is larger and the card padding wider than the mockup's.
- The appointing president shows their full stored name, for example "Dwight D. Eisenhower", where
  the mockup shows "Reagan". Shortening it would mean guessing at names, which this project does
  not do.
