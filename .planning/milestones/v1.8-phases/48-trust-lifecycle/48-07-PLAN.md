<objective>
Turn the single `published_at` promotion into a two-gate decision: a non-overridable
completeness precondition and an overridable trust judgment, with the override recorded
permanently on the transition it authorized.

Purpose: TRUST-04 and TRUST-05 are the phase's user-visible payoff. Without the gate the
tier is decoration; without the logged override the gate is a dead end that makes an
uncertain argument unpublishable. D-14 keeps the two gates distinct so the operator always
knows which wall they hit, and D-17's required reason is what makes "deliberate" real.

Output: the extended service, the request/response contract, the router error mapping, and
the tier on the admin detail endpoint.
</objective>
