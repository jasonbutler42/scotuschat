    Step 1 — Collect distinct person_ids: SELECT DISTINCT person_id FROM utterances WHERE argument_id = argument_id AND person_id IS NOT NULL. Return [] immediately if no person_ids.

    Step 2 — Fetch people + roles in one query: SELECT Person, Role.name.label("role_name") with outerjoin on Role via Person.role_id. Filter Person.id.in_(person_ids).
