| Threat ID | Category | Component | Severity | Disposition | Mitigation Plan |

| T-25-01 | Elevation of privilege | create_person_for_job | high | mitigate | Load job -> argument -> participant by raw_speaker_label and update only rows belonging to that argument. |
| T-25-02 | Tampering | PersonCreate | medium | mitigate | Use Pydantic allow-listed fields only; do not accept arbitrary Person or ArgumentParticipant attributes. |
| T-25-03 | Information disclosure | FailedStepRecovery | low | mitigate | Return human guidance separately from raw error so UI can hide technical details in disclosure. |
| T-25-14 | Elevation of privilege | update_resolve_row_for_job | high | mitigate | Derive argument_id from job_id and update only ArgumentParticipant rows belonging to that argument; reject participant_ids outside the job's argument (IDOR). |
| T-25-15 | Tampering | ResolveRowUpdate | medium | mitigate | Allow-list side and title only; force title null for BENCH; reject edits when argument.status is not pipeline. |
