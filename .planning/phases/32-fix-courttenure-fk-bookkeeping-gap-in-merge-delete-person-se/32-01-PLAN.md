<objective>
Close the CourtTenure FK bookkeeping gap in the three admin-people service functions and their response schema, so merging or deleting a Bench person with tenure rows no longer raises an unhandled IntegrityError (500). Implements requirement PADM-05 (per D-01 through D-04).

Purpose: `CourtTenure.person_id` is `nullable=False` with a plain FK (no ON DELETE CASCADE). None of `get_merge_preview`, `merge_people`, or `delete_person_if_orphan` account for CourtTenure, so any operation on a Justice with tenure rows 500s. This became newly reachable when Phase 27 added full tenure-row CRUD to the People editor.
Output: `MergePreview.tenures` field, CourtTenure added to all 3 service FK loops, and mirrored test coverage for the 5th table.
</objective>
