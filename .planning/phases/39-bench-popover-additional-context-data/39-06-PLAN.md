  <what-built>
Phase 39 is code-complete across five plans:

- Two Alembic migrations: `people.death_date`, and `court_tenures.reason_left` with a CHECK
  constraint allowing only `retired` / `died` / `promoted` or NULL.
- The justices CSV importer now reads `Birthdate`, `Death Date` and `Reason Left`, filling only
  blank fields and never overwriting a value you set by hand.
- The person editor's Death Date field and per-tenure Reason Left dropdown are live.
- The public speaker endpoint now returns birthdate, death date, bio text, and per-tenure
  appointing president, that president's party, and the reason the tenure ended. The old
  always-null top-level appointing-president field is gone.
- The popover card is rebuilt: header row with a role pill, birth/death line, clamped bio with a
  Read more toggle, one three-line block per tenure, and a descriptor slot on advocate cards.
  </what-built>
  <how-to-verify>
Run these on the Windows side (PowerShell, `.venv` activated). Steps 1-3 are the parts nothing
in this phase could do for you.

**1. Apply the migrations.**

    python -m alembic current
    python -m alembic upgrade head
    python -m alembic current

Expect the second `current` to name the reason-left revision as head.

**2. Backfill the historical data.**

    python -m pipeline import-justices

Expect: `0 people created`, `0 court_tenures created`, and non-zero backfill counts for
birthdate, death date and reason.
If it prints a line about unrecognised Reason Left values, note them — the source file changed.

**3. Start the stack.**

    .\scripts\dev-start.ps1

**4. Rehnquist — the two-tenure case.** Open any argument he sat on and click his avatar.
Check:
- A line reading `b. Oct 1, 1924 · d. Sep 3, 2005`.
- Two tenure blocks, most recent first.
- Chief Justice block: date range, then `Ronald Reagan · Republican`, then `Died in office`.
- Associate Justice block: date range, then `Richard Nixon · Republican`, then `Promoted`.
- The whole card fits on screen, or scrolls inside itself with both blocks reachable.

**5. A living Justice.** Click any current Justice's avatar. Check:
- The line shows `b. …` only — no `d. …` half, no dash, no `Unknown`.
- Their current tenure block shows no reason line.

**6. Two Justices appointed by different parties.** Click one appointed by a Democratic
president and one appointed by a Republican president. Check:
- Same field order, same font size, same grey colour on both.
- No red, no blue, no bold, no icon, nothing that differs by party.

**7. The bio toggle.** On a Justice with a long bio:
- The paragraph is cut off at three lines.
- `Read more` appears below it. Click it — the full text shows and the link becomes `Show less`.
- Click again — it collapses.
On a Justice with a short bio: no `Read more` link at all.
On a Justice with no bio: no paragraph and no link.

**8. An advocate.** Click an advocate's avatar. Check:
- Role pill (for example `Petitioner's Counsel`).
- An italic `Coming soon` line under the pill.
- Their bio, if they have one.
- No tenure section.

**9. Nothing extra.** On every card you opened, confirm there is no age, no tenure length, no
case count, and no `Edit person` link. All four are deliberately out of scope.

**10. The editor round-trip.** Go to Admin → People → pick a Justice → open the editor.
- Set a Death Date. Pick a Reason Left on one tenure. Press `Save Person`.
- Reload the page. Both values are still there.
- Now change the Bio text and save the Bio & Photo card.
- Reload again. The Death Date is unchanged.

**11. The dropdown.** On the same editor, open the Reason Left dropdown. It offers exactly:
`— None —`, `Retired`, `Died in office`, `Promoted`.
