<objective>
Close 39-UAT.md gaps 2 and 3. The operator compared the shipped popover
(`../bench popover.png`) against the Figma mockups (`popover - Bench.png`, `popover-Advocate.png`)
and found the separator dot illegible and the overall layout wrong. Direct comparison of the three
images confirms both, and identifies exactly four structural differences:

1. **The separator has no space around it.** Svelte trims whitespace-only text at the start and
   end of an `{#if}` block body, so the padded separator written as inline text inside those
   guards renders flush: the birth/death line loses the space on both sides, the
   president/party line loses the leading one. The mockup's separator is the same small glyph at
   the same size — its legibility comes entirely from the space around it, so the fix is layout,
   not a bigger glyph.
2. **Tenure rows are one line, not two columns.** The mockup renders each tenure as
   `Chief Justice` in semibold near-white on the left with `Sep 1986 – Sep 2005` right-aligned on
   the same line, then `Reagan · Republican` on the left with `Died in office` right-aligned
   beneath. The shipped card renders a single unemphasised line joining title and years with a
   dash, then two more left-aligned lines.
3. **Section dividers are missing.** The mockup separates every stacked section from the one
   above it with a hairline rule; the shipped card has one rule, above the tenure list only. This
   is the largest contributor to "does not look like the design" — without the rules the card
   reads as one undifferentiated grey block, which is also why the name reads as less prominent.
4. **The tenure date range is year-only.** The mockup and `39-CONTEXT.md`'s own recorded mockup
   description both show month-and-year (`Sep 1986 – Sep 2005`, `Jan 1972 – Sep 1986`).
   39-UI-SPEC.md's copywriting row froze the year-only form as "unchanged from current code";
   this plan follows the mockup instead, since mockup fidelity is the gap being closed.

The perceived name/title prominence gap is addressed by restoring hierarchy — a semibold
near-white office title plus the section rules — rather than by inventing a fifth type size, a
third weight or a new colour. The name and role pill themselves already match 39-UI-SPEC.md's
typography table and are left alone. Five deliberate differences from the mockups remain and are
listed for the operator in Plan 39-09 rather than silently changed or silently ignored.

Purpose: the card the operator actually looks at should look like the design they approved.
Output: a mockup-faithful popover, with the layout pinned by a test so a later edit cannot quietly
undo it.
</objective>

**The range helper.** Add two functions beside `formatShort()`, which stays exactly as it is for
the birth/death line:
