<objective>
Make every whole-turn transcription marker read one way and stop discarding the speaker of a
lost-words turn: the importer keeps the canonical label `detect_stage_direction` already returns,
keeps the verbatim form beside it, and treats a whole-turn inaudible marker as that speaker's turn
rather than a room event. Then put both stored rendering facts on the public payload.

Purpose: SPEAKER-06/07/08, and the payload half of SPEAKER-01 — plans 53-03 and 53-04 render only
what this plan puts on the wire. RESEARCH.md found the root cause one function away from the
vocabulary: `_split_turn_into_rows` uses the canonical label as a boolean and re-stores the raw
segment, and the boolean conflates a room event (no speaker, nothing lost) with a transcription
failure (a known speaker's words lost).

Output: a three-way row classification in the importer, the regex trailing-period fix, the
canonical-form helper in the vocabulary module, the two public schema fields, and a regression
matrix covering the 31-form vocabulary's classes and every adjacency/empty/ordering edge.

**Reversibility — `costly`, carried from CONTEXT.md D-10.** Writing the canonical form into
`utterances.text` changes the frozen D-13 content digest of every conversation holding a
whole-turn marker, so a reconcile against a pre-phase database would replace those utterance sets
(and, for a published argument, leave them frozen with a warning). That is acceptable only because
the pre-launch database is reseeded, which is the stated doctrine; the digest module itself is not
touched. Flagged, not gated — the operator chose this door in D-10, and re-asking a locked decision
is itself a defect under CLAUDE.md's Defect Policy.

**Claude's-discretion call recorded here (Defect Policy — correctness, not taste):** an
inaudible-only turn with no `speaker` key at all falls back to today's unattributed stage-direction
treatment (canonical text, verbatim kept). D-04 governs a *known* speaker; with no speaker the only
honest rendering is "absent renders as absent" — dropping the row would lose content and borrowing a
neighbour's speaker would invent it. The measured corpus has zero such turns (13,221 known + 5,860
sentinel = all 19,081), so this is a defensive branch.

**Flagged planner assumption (unresolved edge-probe row — carried, not dropped):**
- **SPEAKER-07 / unclassified** — the probe could not classify it. Covered by the D-04 and Pitfall 1
  truths above; the probe row itself stays unresolved.
</objective>
