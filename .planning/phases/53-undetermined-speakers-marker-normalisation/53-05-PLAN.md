<objective>
Say why a majority-undetermined argument is held, with its number, on every admin surface that
explains a blocked publish.

Purpose: D-18. Plan 53-01 already puts `{code: "majority_undetermined_speaker", count, percent}` on
the blocker payload that reaches all three surfaces (the publish-attempt 422 on both arguments pages,
and `summarize_tier_blockers` on every review-queue row — RESEARCH.md Open Question 2). What is
missing is the sentence.

Output: `blockerSentence` extracted from its three byte-identical copies into one tested module
(verified during planning: the three function bodies hash identically), the locked D-18 branch
added once, and all three pages rendering through it.

**Why extract instead of editing three copies (Claude's discretion).** The copies' own comments say
they are duplicated "so both pages degrade identically"; one module guarantees that instead of
hoping for it, and it makes the sentence testable as a pure function with `node --test`, following
the `app/src/lib/admin/resetOutcome.js` + `app/tests/reset-outcome-classifier.test.mjs` precedent —
a behaviour test of a function, not a source-text grep of a `.svelte` file (Testing Policy).

**Rounding (Claude's discretion, decided in 53-01):** the backend sends half-up integer percent; the
frontend prints it verbatim. An argument at 50.2% therefore reads "50% of turns have an undetermined
speaker (more than half)." — the parenthetical carries the fact the rounded number cannot.
</objective>
