## How it was found

The admin People → "Justices with tenure gaps" filter flagged Thurgood Marshall (tenure
1967-10-02 → 1991-10-01). The flagged argument is *Baltimore & Ohio R.R. Co. v. United States*
(ConvoKit `1966_642`, argued 1967-01-09): Marshall was Solicitor General, and his first turn opens
"Mr. Chief Justice, may it please the Court. The position of the United States Government is…".
The source credits all 62 turns to `j__thurgood_marshall` (type J); his advocate id
`thurgood_marshall` (type A) exists in `speakers.json` but carries zero utterances.

A full scan of justice-id utterances against DB tenure (term-year granularity — undercounts the
term a justice joined) found three classes:

| Class | Examples | Scale |
|---|---|---|
| Pre-appointment advocacy credited to the justice id | Roberts (39 cases / 1,800 turns), Marshall (21 cases incl. NAACP LDF 1957–60 and SG 1965–67), Ginsburg, Alito, Stevens, Powell, Burger, Rehnquist, Kagan, Blackmun | ~3,500 turns |
| Wrong justice | Harlan I carrying Harlan II | 48 cases / 803 turns |
| Post-tenure / impossible | Black, Douglas, Frankfurter, Burton, Reed, Clark, Stewart, O'Connor, Harlan II (2003) | 1–42 turns each |

Scan script (throwaway): scratchpad `tenure_scan.py` of session 2026-09-30.

## Decisions

- **D-A — Three classes, three corrections.** Pre-appointment → same person, advocate side.
  Wrong justice → the single same-surname justice sitting on that date; zero or several → refuse
  to guess. Impossible → undetermined speaker (Phase 53 Treatment D).
- **D-B — The advocate card for a future justice carries a short factual note** that the person
  later became a justice and this argument predates the appointment (not final wording — roughly
  "became a justice in {year}; this is a case they argued before then"). Operator's reason, which
  is the test for the wording: it answers the confusion a reader has when they recognise the name
  ("wait, I thought Ruth Bader Ginsburg was a justice…") — faithful disambiguation, not added
  weight. Exact copy is an operator decision at UI time.
- **D-C — Own phase, before bulk publish.** Operator chose a dedicated inserted phase over folding
  it into Phase 54; Phase 54 depends on 53.1.
